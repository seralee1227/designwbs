## What color palette or theming trends (color schemes, dark mode approaches, gradient styles) are trending?

### Takeaway
No color-trend news specific to Nov 6–13, 2026 surfaced. The dominant, well-documented signal is Canva's third annual Design Trends Report (published **December 2025**, so roughly 11 months old relative to today, not new this week) naming 2026 the year of "Imperfect by Design" — a reaction against flat, AI-generated sameness (identical gradients, same purple-to-blue color default, glassmorphism) toward tactile, textured, more human-handmade palettes. This "anti-AI-sameness" framing is the most concrete, citable throughline connecting typography, color, and layout discussion in 2026, but it is an ongoing 2026-long narrative, not a this-week event.

### Cited Findings
- Canva's "Design Trends Report" (3rd annual edition) was published in **December 2025** and named "Imperfect by Design" as the year's core 2026 theme; it reports a 90% rise in searches for DIY-inspired elements, a 220% jump in searches for "liminal"/"uncanny" content (tied to a sub-trend called "Reality Warp"), a 30% rise in searches for tactile/realistic textures, and that 80% of surveyed creators (1,000 creators surveyed across the US and Brazil) say 2026 is "the year to regain creative control" — [eCommerceNews NZ](https://ecommercenews.co.nz/story/canva-backs-imperfect-by-design-trend-in-2026-report); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210696597/en); [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/2026-is-the-year-of-imperfect-by-design/506629); [Mediaweek Australia](https://mediaweek.com.au/data/why-designs-next-flex-is-being-imperfect)
- "AI slop" is described as a recognized industry term for generic AI-default visuals: "the same Inter font, the same purple-to-blue gradient, and the same rounded-corner card layout," with designers reacting via code brutalism, mixed-media maximalism, and lo-fi/retro revival — [925studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-web-design-guide); [925studios on AI-tell fonts/gradients](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells)

- Multiple 2026 color-trend blog posts converge on: texture/dimension replacing flat solid color; warm neutrals and earthy tones (beige bases with olive/terracotta accents) for a "calm, nature-connected" palette; restrained bright accents used only on CTAs/links; deep teal and jewel-tone palettes (e.g., a named "Transformative Teal #1F5C63 / Deep Moss #5F6F52 / Cocoa Brown #4A3A32" combination); a "Mermaidcore" palette of iridescent aqua, soft teal, pearlescent purple, glittery silver; and continued neon-on-dark "dark mode maximalism" — [Davey & Krista 2026 brand color trends](https://daveyandkrista.com/2026-brand-color-trends/); [Vistaprint top 8 colour trends 2026](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/color-trends); [LoungeLizard web design color trends](https://www.loungelizard.com/blog/web-design-color-trends/)

## What layout/grid trends (bento grids, asymmetric layouts, maximalism vs minimalism) are appearing in recent site launches or design roundups?

### Takeaway
Bento grids remain the single most consistently cited 2026 layout pattern — described by multiple sources as "the undisputed layout standard for 2026" — but this is a continuing/established trend (popularized by Apple, now used by Google, Microsoft, Spotify, and widely in B2B SaaS homepages) rather than something new this week. Alongside it, 2026 commentary describes a convergence toward "soft" or "controlled" maximalism: minimalist structural grids carrying maximalist visual personality (layered visuals, expressive type, purposeful motion) rather than either pure minimalism or pure maximalism winning outright.

### Cited Findings
- Bento grids are called "the undisputed layout standard for 2026" by one roundup, while another lists them among five already-established (not newly emerging) 2026 trends; a third says bento grids remain "a staple in 2026, just as it was in 2025" — [Line25 Web Design Trends 2026](https://line25.com/articles/web-design-trends-2026/); [aggregated bento-grid search synthesis, Nov 2026]
- Bento grids are specifically called out as a "scanning behavior fix that B2B SaaS sites are quietly standardising on," with a recommended "sweet spot of 6–9 tiles" before the layout starts to feel cluttered — [Pravinkumar: bento grids B2B SaaS homepage trend](https://www.pravinkumar.co/blog/bento-grids-b2b-saas-homepage-design-trend-2026)
- A caution from the same body of commentary: bento grids "collapse badly on mobile and break reading order for screen readers," so they're best suited to feature/dashboard pages rather than full-site adoption — [aggregated search synthesis citing vendor guidance]
- CSS Grid is described as the standard implementation method, called "the only layout system that offers true two-dimensional control over both rows and columns" for bento-style layouts — [aggregated search synthesis]
- Multiple 2026 trend guides describe a shift toward "Soft Maximalism" / "Maximalism With Control," framed as "Minimalist Structure + Maximalist Personality" — layered visuals, expressive typography, and purposeful (not distracting) motion/animation layered onto otherwise-clean grids — [Acodez maximalist web design](https://acodez.in/maximalist-web-design/); [iManila re-emergence of maximalist web design](https://imanila.ph/2026/01/12/the-re-emergence-of-maximalist-web-design-and-what-it might-mean); [Zozothemes minimalist vs maximalist 2026](https://zozothemes.com/minimalist-vs-maximalist-web-design/)
- Minimalism is far from dead: several sources argue "intent-driven minimalism with clarity-first layouts" remains a major, parallel 2026 approach, especially recommended for professional/tech-focused brands, with maximalism reserved for brands wanting emotional storytelling/standout personality — [aggregated minimalism-vs-maximalism search synthesis]
- Separately, "brutalism and maximalism are making a monumental comeback in 2026" per one trend guide, while a different vendor's own (unverified) internal data claims brutalism's demand "peaked in March 2026 and is declining" — a direct contradiction flagged as such, with no independent data to resolve it — [Reallygooddesigns web design trends 2026](https://reallygooddesigns.com/web-design-trends-2026/) vs. [unnamed vendor 2026 trend-demand data, unverifiable]

### Inferences
- The layout conversation in 2026 is less about a single winning grid pattern and more about blending a disciplined grid (bento or otherwise) with maximalist decorative/motion layers — this hybrid framing recurs across independent-seeming blogs enough to be a genuine cross-source pattern, even if each individual source is a vendor/agency blog with promotional interest.
- Brutalism's trajectory (rising vs. peaking-and-declining) is contested and not resolved by available sources — a final report should present this as an open disagreement rather than pick a side.
