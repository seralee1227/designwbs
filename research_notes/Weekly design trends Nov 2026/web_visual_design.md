## What visual design trends are design blogs/publications highlighting in November 2026?

### Takeaway
No major publication (Smashing Magazine, Awwwards blog, CSS-Tricks) produced a dated, citable November 2026 trend piece in search results; coverage is dominated by generic "2026 trends" roundups (many from agency/vendor blogs) rather than publication-dated reporting. The clearest *recency-dated* signal is a wave of commentary — from UX Collective newsletter issues and design blogs — explicitly naming "AI design slop" (default purple gradients, oversized border radius, generic "Live" badges) as a visible pattern designers are now reacting against, with bento grids, brutalism/anti-design, and variable/display type as the three recurring layout-and-type threads across nearly all 2026 roundups.

### Inferences
- The strongest *recency* signal this week is not a new aesthetic but a naming/backlash moment: designers and publications are now explicitly cataloguing "AI slop" visual tells (purple gradients, oversized corner radii, generic badges) as a thing to deliberately avoid — which is pushing brutalist/anti-design and more idiosyncratic typography as differentiation strategies.
- Bento grids appear to be reaching "mainstream/safe" status (high adoption claims, SaaS-specific playbooks) at the same time a counter-trend (anti-grid brutalism) is forming specifically in opposition to them — suggesting the field is bifurcating between "polished SaaS default" and "deliberately raw" camps rather than converging on one look.

## What do recent "design trends 2026/2027" roundup articles say?

### Takeaway
2026 roundups (mostly agency/vendor blogs, treated as directional rather than authoritative) converge on: bold/oversized display typography, variable fonts as default infrastructure, bento-grid layouts, a brutalism/anti-design counter-current, and AI-native considerations (designing for both humans and AI crawlers/agents); early 2027 roundups pivot toward "texture," "craft," muted/earthy color and a reaction against polish, though 2027 color-of-the-year signals have not yet been officially set.

### Cited Findings
- DesignRush's "17 Web Design Trends for 2026" frames the year's throughline as: sites must be "fast enough to convert, structured enough for AI, and distinctive enough to not look machine-generated" — [Source](https://www.designrush.com/agency/website-design-development/trends/web-design-trends)

- Reallygooddesigns describes a move toward interactive 3D web design where shapes react to scroll, hover, or motion — [Source](https://reallygooddesigns.com/web-design-trends-2026/)
- Elementor's roundup calls design tokens "the backbone of scalable design systems" across the web in 2026 — [Source](https://elementor.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026/)
- Lovart's "Color, Typography and Branding Trends 2027" piece reports that Behr named "Grounded" (an olive green moving through moss/olive/earth tones) as its 2027 Color of the Year, and that Pantone had not yet named a 2027 color as of October 2026 (Pantone typically announces in December) — [Source](https://www.lovart.ai/blog/color-typography-branding-trends-2027) — Caveat: Lovart sells AI design tools and its pages mix in unverifiable "Q3 2027" statistics; treat only the Behr/Pantone-status claims as reasonably solid, not Lovart's own forward projections.
