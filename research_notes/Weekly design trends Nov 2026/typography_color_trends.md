## What new typefaces, font releases, or type foundries/tools got attention recently?

### Cited Findings
- Swiss Typefaces completed a custom, "warmer, friendlier" typeface for Swiss Post, a project reported as having taken three years — covered by Creative Boom on 2 November 2026 — [Creative Boom](https://creativeboom.com/work/swiss-typefaces-crafts-a-warmer-friendlier-custom-font-for-swiss-post)

- Pangram Pangram relaunched its own foundry website with an "editorial-inspired redesign" that puts its typefaces center stage — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/news/pangram-pangrams-new-website-puts-its-typefaces-centre-stage-with-a-refined-editorial-inspired-redesign/)

## What color palette trends or color tools are design publications/social media highlighting this week?

### Takeaway
There is no new, dated-this-week color trend report; the dominant color conversation in late November 2026 is still the aftermath of Pantone's 2026 Cloud Dancer (white/neutral) pick and speculation about the still-unannounced 2027 Color of the Year, set against a broader 2026 forecast consensus that favors warmer, richer, more saturated tones (contradicting Pantone's neutral choice).

### Cited Findings
- As of 26 November 2026, Pantone has not announced its 2027 Color of the Year; Pantone's own site promotes a 2027 program with events in four cities but withholds the shade, and a Pantone executive explicitly declined to hint at it in an interview — [Homes & Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/decor/paint/pantone-color-of-the-year-2027-predictions)
- Designers surveyed by Homes & Gardens are predicting the 2027 Color of the Year, with guesses including olive green and a jewel-toned green — explicitly framed as speculation, not confirmed — [Homes & Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/decor/paint/pantone-color-of-the-year-2027-predictions)
- Pantone's 2026 Color of the Year, Cloud Dancer (PANTONE 11-4201), a white/neutral shade, was announced December 2025 and has drawn commentary/pushback for being a muted, "whisper of tranquility" choice at a time when other forecasters are calling for bolder color — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/12/04/nx-s1-5632651/pantones-color-of-the-year-2026-white); [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/pantone-color-of-the-year-tech-pushback)
- Pantone separately published its NYFW Spring/Summer 2027 color trend report naming its top 10 fashion colors (dated September 2026) — a distinct, fashion-specific forecast from the Color of the Year program — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/news/fashion/pantone-nyfw-spring-summer-2027-color-trend-report-the-top-10-colors/2026092174743)
- Broader 2026 forecast consensus across design/interiors outlets favors warm, saturated, and "joyful" color after several years of muted beige/neutral palettes: Heuritech's AI-driven monthly picks include Cinnamon, Purple Red, Tan, and Razzmic Berry; Vistaprint's "top 8" trends for 2026 include Mermaidcore, Banana Yellow, and Tangerine Disco, explicitly framed as a shift away from muted beige; Lummi's list emphasizes rich burgundy, terracotta, "transformative teal," and deep purples/warm violets — [Heuritech](https://heuritech.com/articles/color-trends/); [Vistaprint](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/color-trends); [Lummi](https://lummi.ai/blog/2026-color-trends)
- Counter-trend: Pinterest Predicts 2026 names "glacier blue" and "cool blue" as top shades, framed around "subzero sophistication" — a cooler-toned forecast that runs against the warm-earth-tone consensus from other sources — [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/colour-trends-2026)
- WGSN's "Transformative Teal" and Behr's "Hidden Gem" (a smoky jade) continue to be cited as leading color-of-the-year-adjacent picks for 2026 — [Jukebox Print roundup via search summary]
- No Coolors-branded 2026 trend report (e.g., a "Coolors predicts" or similar palette-tool-specific publication) could be found in this research session.

### Inferences
- The color conversation right now is less about a fresh "this week" discovery and more about an ongoing tension between (a) Pantone's institutionally cautious, neutral 2026 pick and backlash to it, and (b) a louder commercial/retail chorus (Vistaprint, Heuritech, Lummi) pushing warmer, more saturated, maximalist color for 2026–2027.
- The 2027 Color of the Year announcement (typically made in late November/early December based on the 2026 Cloud Dancer reveal timing of Dec 4, 2025) is likely imminent — within days to a couple weeks of this research date — making it the single most anticipated color-trend event on the near-term horizon, but it has not happened yet as of Nov 26, 2026.
